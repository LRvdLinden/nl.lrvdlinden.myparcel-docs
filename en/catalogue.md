# Carriers

**78 carriers in the catalogue, 21 of them already in Homey** (19 devices; Intelcom is part of Dragonfly and BRT part of DPD). The other carriers are in our reference catalogue, labelled **Coming soon**.

[**Open the interactive carrier catalogue and Details popups →**](https://lrvdlinden.github.io/nl.lrvdlinden.myparcel-docs/carriers/)

Cards include Account / Tracking / API labels, countries, an availability filter and Capabilities detail popups. For carriers that are not available yet, the connection type is indicative.
