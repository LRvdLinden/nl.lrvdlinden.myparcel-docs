# Install and connect

1. In Homey open Devices → Add device → MyParcel, choose your carrier and follow its sign-in screen. Add a separate device for each carrier you use.
2. Allow parcel data to sync.
3. Add the MyParcel widgets to your Homey Dashboard and choose the devices to display.

Available data depends on the carrier and shipment. Not every field is populated for every parcel.
