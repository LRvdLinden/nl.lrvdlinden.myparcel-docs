# bpost

![bpost](../../media/drivers/bpost/assets/images/large.png)

**Region:** 🇧🇪 Belgium

**Description:** My bpost

## Install and connect

In Homey open Devices → Add device → MyParcel, choose your carrier and follow its sign-in screen. Add a separate device for each carrier you use.

## Device data

* `bpost_parcel_count`
* `bpost_total_count`
* `bpost_status`
* `bpost_tracking`
* `bpost_sender`
* `bpost_delivery_date`
* `bpost_delivery_window`
* `bpost_delivery_point`
* `bpost_weight`
* `bpost_product`
* `bpost_partner`
* `bpost_last_event`
* `bpost_account_status`
* `bpost_last_update`

Available data depends on the carrier and shipment. Not every field is populated for every parcel.
