# InPost UK

![InPost UK](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LRvdLinden/nl.lrvdlinden.myparcel/main/drivers/inpost-uk/assets/images/large.png)

**Region:** 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

**Description:** InPost UK parcels

## Install and connect

In Homey open Devices → Add device → MyParcel, choose your carrier and follow its sign-in screen. Add a separate device for each carrier you use.

## Device data

* `inpost_uk_parcel_count`
* `inpost_uk_status`
* `myparcel_connection_status`
* `inpost_uk_last_update`

Available data depends on the carrier and shipment. Not every field is populated for every parcel.
