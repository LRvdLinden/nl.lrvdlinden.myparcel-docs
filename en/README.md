# Welcome

![MyParcel](../media/assets/images/large.png)

MyParcel brings your parcel carriers together in Homey. Add a device per carrier and see your deliveries, device values, widgets and automations in one place. This documentation covers **MyParcel v0.3.7**.

## What you get

* **One device per carrier** with active parcels, status, sender, delivery date and window, out for delivery/ready for pickup and last event, among others – see [Device data](device.md).
* **Flow cards** for new parcel, status change, out for delivery, ready for pickup, delivered and problems, plus conditions and actions to track parcels – see [Flow cards](flows.md) and [Flow tokens](tokens.md).
* **Dashboard widgets**: MyParcel Packages, MyParcel Delivery, the PostNL widgets and the German DHL/Deutsche Post widgets – see [Widgets](widgets.md).
* **13 languages**: English, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Swedish, Norwegian, Spanish, Danish, Russian, Polish, Korean and Arabic (right-to-left). Dates, times, weights and dimensions follow your language.
